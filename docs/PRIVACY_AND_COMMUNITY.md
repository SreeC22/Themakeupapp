# Stage 1 privacy and community boundary

## Stage 2 update

The newer owner brief excludes public community from the MVP until 500+ registered users and explicit phase approval. Private drafts and compatibility data remain preserved, not publicly posted or moderated.

Profiles/saves/drafts still persist locally. Selected traits, catalog reference IDs, fit/oxidation feedback and category preferences are now processed transiently by the recommendation API, without a server profile or request-body logging. Private draft prose is not sent. The UI discloses this network processing; older Stage 1 “nothing is submitted” wording is historical, not the current matching-data policy.

## Historical Stage 1 boundary

The website preview has no real member accounts, public review feed, moderator authority or cloud synchronization. Local saves and draft experiences must be labeled as such. A demonstration moderation status is not an actual submission or decision.

Anonymous quiz answers and optional draft text can be stored only in the current browser, with visible disclosure, a private default, export and confirmed deletion controls. Local storage is not encrypted account storage and can be accessible to other people using the same browser profile. Do not put private information in a preview draft. Existing legacy storage keys must not be erased.

No new tracking, photographs, camera permissions, face inference, diagnoses, ethnicity, race, birthdate or gender inputs are authorized. Optional complexion and pigmentation information is self-reported, not a medical judgment. Do not send it to analytics.

Real community evidence remains empty until genuine consenting members submit it through a verified backend. Never manufacture people, quotes, counts, rewards or purchase badges. Self-reported product relationships are not verified purchases or proof of independence.

Before Stage 4/5 beta: verify ownership and private saves with two accounts; add reporting, appeals, moderation roles, rate limits and deletion/retention controls; draft owner/legal-reviewed privacy and community policies. No public profile sharing or review publication is implied by the local privacy preference.
