# Stage 3 — Your Neu Look

## Confirmed core product direction

Neu is a **personalized makeup look builder**, not primarily a foundation shade-matching tool.

Primary journey:

**Beauty Profile → Choose a Look → Set Budget → Generate Personalized Makeup Set → Customize → Save or Shop**

Complexion matching is supporting infrastructure. Individual-product discovery remains available, alongside the completed Stage 1/2 features and data safeguards.

## Implementation sequence within this stage

1. Align product direction and introduce evidence-bounded look planning.
2. Build mobile-first occasion/budget generation and independent slot customization.
3. Add owned-product reuse, complete/draft saved looks, editing and lossless local-data protections.
4. Expand evidence strategically, run automated and browser checks, and present coverage/limitations for review.

These are implementation checkpoints within the authorized Stage 3, not permission to start subsequent stages.

## Required experience

- Four initial occasions: Everyday, Soft Glam, Party Glam, Work & College.
- Four explicit slots: Foundation, Concealer, Blush, Lips.
- Use the saved complexion, undertone, skin type, exact references, preferences and verified evidence.
- Offer overall USD budget and/or editorial budget tier. Do not convert tier labels into invented product prices.
- Swap only the chosen slot; do not regenerate other choices.
- Mark and reuse owned products. Ownership and user-reported fit remain distinct from verified manufacturer facts.
- Save, reopen, edit and remove named complete/draft looks locally, preserving choices and unresolved slots.
- Explain choices and missing evidence. Occasion recipes are aesthetic guidance, not tested compatibility or manufacturer wear-time claims.
- Link actual internal product details and observed official US manufacturer/retailer pages. No fabricated merchant links or availability.
- Keep browsing products individually, existing two-path onboarding, reference editing, legacy IDs/storage, comparisons, product saves, Learn and private feedback.

## Evidence and data integrity

No fabricated matches, combinations, prices, compatibility, availability, users or percentages. If four supported choices cannot be formed, retain the unresolved slots and explain what is missing.

Unknown prices are not zero. Owned-product reuse has zero **new purchase** cost, not a fabricated retail price. Verified quoted prices remain dated observations, not checkout totals, tax/shipping estimates or current-stock guarantees. A numeric budget cannot be declared satisfied if any selected new purchase lacks a verified price.

Existing source freshness, hard undertone/olive conflicts, exact reference identities, uncertainty, correction handling, relational constraints and source-observation isolation remain in force. Expansion is additive; prior records/evidence must not be deleted or silently upgraded.

The 25-brand / 100–150-line / approximately 2,000–4,000-variant target remains progressive. Prioritize complexion/formula gaps and source-described lip/cheek colors that support usable looks now. Complete current US ranges remain the eventual per-line goal; partial ranges/traits are reported separately.

## Storage and privacy

Guest-first, browser-local looks and owned products; no account gate or cloud-save claim. Protect corrupt/future data, report failed writes, preserve unsaved edits on conflicts, revalidate saved choices without silently replacing them, and integrate export/confirmed reset. Do not send private look names, private feedback prose or owned-item notes to recommendation processing.

## Exit gate

Provide a working preview, actual test results, current catalog coverage and known limitations. No public community. Stop for review. Do not push or deploy.
