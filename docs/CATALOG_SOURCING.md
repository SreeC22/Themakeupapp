# Stage 1 catalog provenance

The preview projects sixteen existing historical records, four per launch category, from `research/neu-catalog/initial.json`. The source observations were checked on 2026-10-17.

The projection retains recorded brand, product name, category, source URL, product URL, date and original evidence note. It does not reverify current retailer destinations or availability. Previous research specifically warns that returned items and URL slugs can disagree; Stage 2 must recheck both product identity and current destinations.

Prices, finish, coverage and exact-shade facts are unknown. No SKU, color hex, product photograph, stock claim, suitability claim or review was generated. Named product-card evidence is not evidence for a shade recommendation.

The source collection contains many unreviewed brand candidates and broader categories. A candidate directory is not a verified product catalog or a claim of complete category coverage. The proposed 25-brand shortlist remains an owner-reviewed Stage 2 editorial plan, not sixteen preview products padded into a complete catalog.

Stage 2 must reverify each listing with permitted sources, record usage/image permissions and exact variants, and show a 25-brand coverage matrix with honest gaps. Do not scrape Reddit reviews or proprietary Pantone values. No supplier account, paid feed, new scraping process or current-inventory API is used for this preview.
