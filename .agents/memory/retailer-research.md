---
name: Retailer research limits
description: Interpret retailer page access and product identities without inventing evidence.
---

Treat returned retailer page content as evidence, not the URL's product-name slug or an empty result.

**Why:** Target can return the product tied to a numeric item ID even when the text slug names a different product. Its directory/brand pages can return navigation shells without the assortment. These responses do not prove category offerings or absence.

**How to apply:** Confirm actual product headings/cards and brand identity. Record inaccessible or incomplete directories as research gaps. Keep unknown separate from explicit unavailability, and never describe observed retailer minimums as total US market counts.
