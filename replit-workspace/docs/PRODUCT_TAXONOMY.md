# Stage 1 product taxonomy

Shopping categories are exactly Foundation, Concealer, Blush and Lips. Lips includes cataloged lipstick, tint, gloss, stain and liner. Prior makeup history can describe a skin tint or BB cream without creating extra shopping categories.

Complexion depth, undertone, olive subtype, saturation, surface redness, skin type and lip pigmentation are separate self-described dimensions. Unknown is a valid value. Redness does not imply a cool undertone or a diagnosis.

Concealer intent distinguishes spot concealment, under-eye brightening and correction. Foundation shade candidates, concealer intent, blush color-family exploration and lip opacity goals are not interchangeable recommendation concepts.

The existing backend research taxonomy is broader and remains intact. Stage 1 uses a narrow read-only projection from its historical product observations; no schema migration or destructive category rewrite is performed.
