The heart of the Neu feed: a product shown through your tone twins, with their verdicts. Composes `Badge` + `Avatar`.

```jsx
<ProductCard
  brand="Rare Beauty" name="Soft Pinch Blush" price="$23"
  image="var(--berry-soft)"
  verdicts={["Didn't go muddy","Buildable"]}
  twins={{ count: 214, tone: 7 }}
/>
```
