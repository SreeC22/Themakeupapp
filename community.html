THE NEU COLOR NOTEBOOK

Depth and undertone ask different questions.

Depth describes how light or deep a shade family appears. Undertone describes a subtle temperature or cast. Warm, cool and neutral can appear at any depth. Olive is a muted green-gold cast that can sit alongside any of them.

Warm · golden, peachCool · pink, blueNeutral · a balanceOlive · muted green-gold

For color, neighboring hues can feel tonal. Opposite hues can add contrast. Skin depth changes contrast, not permission. There is no single correct palette.
